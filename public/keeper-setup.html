Set up this device

Do this once, on the tablet, phone or other device your loved one will use. Afterwards it opens straight to their own pages — no password, ever.

You'll find the code on their profile page, under “Set up their device”.

Is this their device?

This device will stay signed in as them. Only set this up on a device they use themselves.

This device is theirs now

It will open to their pages every time. They never have to sign in.

How big should the words be?

Tap one and the whole device changes. You can change it again any time from the menu.

One last thing — add it to the home screen so it's one tap:

iPad or iPhone: tap the share button, then “Add to Home Screen”.

Android: tap the three dots, then “Add to Home screen”.
