What this is

A first group, before anyone else.

Founding families are the families MemoriGems is shaped around. You are not testing a finished thing — you are the reason parts of it will change.

We have built what we believe helps. What we don’t yet know is how it holds up inside a real family’s week — on a hard afternoon, on a device someone else set up, when nobody feels like recording anything.

That is what the founding families are for. You use it, and we listen closely. When something is awkward, we change it while it’s still small enough to change.

Being honest with you

The part we’d want said to us.

MemoriGems is made by a company. HuLux Innovations LLC built it, and we would like it to succeed. We are telling you that plainly because families facing a diagnosis have been sold hope before.

It is not medical care, and it is not a treatment. It will not slow anything down or bring anything back. We built it the way we did because of what is known about voice, emotion and memory — but that is the reason for the design, not a promise about your family.

You can stop at any point, without explaining yourself. Pausing, stopping or leaving entirely costs you nothing, and your place is never given away. You still receive everything your family made.

Your recordings belong to you. They are not shared outside your family, they are not sold, and they are not used to advertise anything. If MemoriGems stopped existing tomorrow, your family’s voices would not stop existing with it.

None of this replaces the people already helping you. If you need someone tonight, the Alzheimer’s Association helpline is open every hour of every day on 800‑272‑3900.

The pilot itself

What is a MemoriGems pilot?

A small group of families, all using MemoriGems at the same time with someone they love, while we watch closely and change what isn’t working. You are not testing a finished thing — you are the reason parts of it will change. There is nothing to score and nothing you can get wrong.

When does the pilot begin? When does it end?

Mid‑September to mid‑November — about eight weeks. Every family starts together rather than trickling in, which is what lets us keep the group small and give each family real attention.

What happens after the pilot? Does it all vanish?

No. The app closes at the end of the pilot while we build what comes next — but everything your family made is gathered into one place and handed to you: the audio and photos as real files, with a page that plays them on any computer, with no account and no internet. Yours to revisit, for as long as you keep it. You will not need MemoriGems, or us, to open it again.

When MemoriGems opens more widely, founding families hear from us first and keep a founding rate.

How much time does this take?

Two or three hours across the whole eight weeks, and most of that a few minutes at a time. There is no quota, no streak and nothing that counts down at you.

What do you need from me during the pilot?

Two things. Use it as life allows — and tell us the truth about it. That means a short check‑in each week, where a few sentences is plenty, and one longer conversation at the end. Nobody is graded, and a week where you sent nothing is worth telling us about too.

Can I stop and come back? What if I need a break?

Yes, and you don’t owe us a reason. Pause, stop, or pull out entirely — nobody is penalised and your place is not given away. Life happens when you are caring for someone, and we are not here to make it harder. However far you got, you still receive everything your family made.

What if I’m not ready for this?

Sitting down to record your voice for someone whose memory is changing is not a small thing, and plenty of people find they can’t on the first day. Don’t force it. If you want to be in the pilot anyway and start slowly, say so — and if you would rather wait for another time, tell us that instead. Knowing why a family held back is worth as much to us as anything a family sends.

The person you’re recording for

What if it’s too late, or they don’t remember who I am?

That is often the moment MemoriGems is for. Names and faces can go before voices do — families tell us the sound of someone familiar lands when a photograph on its own does not. It does not fix anything. It gives you something to offer on a day when you have run out of things to say.

What if they’re in a care facility or memory care?

That works. Two ways in: they have a phone or tablet of their own that they can use, or someone who loves them visits and opens the Gems together on a visitor’s device. Both are real ways to be part of the pilot — and the second one is worth saying out loud, because sitting beside someone while a Gem plays is not a lesser version of this.

Does the person receiving Gems need an app, or an account?

No. A Gem arrives as a link they can open on any phone or computer. On a tablet you set it up once with a code — no password, nothing for them to remember, nothing for them to install.

Will it interrupt them?

No. Nothing buzzes, rings or emails. A Gem waits quietly until they open their page, which is deliberate — a device that demands attention is the last thing a person with memory changes needs.

Practical things

Do I need a specific tablet or phone? Does it have to be an iPad?

No. A reasonably current phone, tablet or computer with a web browser is enough — iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows or Mac. There is nothing to install, and it does need an internet connection to send and to open a Gem.

One honest gap: we have not tested an Amazon Fire tablet yet. If that is the device you have, tell us when you write — we would rather test it with you before you start than have you find out the hard way.

What if I’m not good with technology?

Then we set it up with you, on a call, and stay on the line until a first Gem has actually arrived. The hardest part of MemoriGems is deciding what to say, not working the app — recording is one button, and you can throw a recording away and start again as many times as you like.

Who else can see what we record?

Only the people your family adds to the circle. We can see how often the app is used — that is how we learn what to fix — and we tell you plainly that we do. The recordings themselves stay inside your family.

What does it cost?

Nothing during the founding families pilot. What it costs afterwards has not been decided yet — and we would rather tell you that than put up a number we are not ready to stand behind. Founding families will hear it from us first, and keep a founding rate.

Us

Who will contact me if I join the pilot?

Both of us. Lisette and Edwina — the two people who built MemoriGems — are your point of contact for the whole eight weeks: setting up, questions, and anything that goes wrong. There is no support queue and no handover to somebody else. That is the reason the pilot is deliberately small.

Who are you, actually? Who is HuLux Innovations?

MemoriGems is made by HuLux Innovations LLC, a small company working on human‑centered communication in dementia and elder care. It was built by two people who have sat in these rooms themselves, and the work behind it was presented at the Alzheimer’s Association International Conference in July 2026.
